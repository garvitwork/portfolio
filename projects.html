01 Computer Vision · AgriTech · MLOps

CropGuard — Crop Disease & Outbreak Intelligence

Farmers snap one leaf photo and get a diagnosis, weather-based risk, and a treatment plan in English and Marathi — while officials track outbreaks live.

End-to-end app: a MobileNetV2 classifier (PlantVillage) served via FastAPI, live weather risk from Open-Meteo, and LLM advisories (Groq with Gemini fallback) with voice output. An officials dashboard flags outbreak clusters when 3+ farmers report the same disease in one district within 6 hours.

Why it matters

Farmers usually spot disease only after damage spreads, and wrong self-diagnosis means wasted pesticide and lost crops. CropGuard gives instant, local-language guidance and turns individual scans into an early-warning signal for extension officers.
